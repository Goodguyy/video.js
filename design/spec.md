# Afroclip Cinema

Proposed direction for approval. A contemporary African cinema skin with charcoal surfaces, warm amber highlights, and a restrained woven pattern along the control rail. Keep the footage clear of decorative elements.

- Put a full-width timeline above the controls. Follow it with play/pause, mute and volume, elapsed and total time, settings, and fullscreen. Use the same order for keyboard navigation and maintain essential controls at narrow widths.
- Use matching rounded icons and a clean sans-serif with tabular numerals for time. Give menus the same charcoal surface, amber selection, and visible focus treatment as the controls. Preserve Escape dismissal and return focus to the trigger.
- Use an amber played track and a clear seek handle. Keep the rail opaque enough for text and controls to stay legible over bright and dark footage. Use container queries and comfortable touch targets.
- Keep transitions brief and reduce them when requested by the operating system. Preserve the official hotkeys, gestures, error dialog, and screen-reader announcements.
