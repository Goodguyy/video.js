# Afroclip Cinema

Approved contest direction. A screening room for contemporary African shorts. The footage has a clean frame above a charcoal control rail, with warm amber light and a geometric weave that belongs to the seek bar.

## The woven timeline

A quiet interlocking diamond pattern runs just below a continuous, high-contrast seek track. As playback advances, the woven pattern lights up behind the playhead. The amber played line, neutral unplayed line, and solid playhead show progress independently of the pattern. Where the video provides chapter cues, the seek preview shows the chapter title and time. Unchaptered clips show the time alone. The weave simplifies at narrow sizes and adds no tab stops.

## Controls and states

The full-width timeline comes first, followed by play/pause, mute and volume, elapsed and total time, settings, and fullscreen. The row stays visible on phones, with a volume popover where space is tight. Settings use the same charcoal surface, cream labels, and amber selection as the rail. Escape dismisses menus and returns focus to the trigger. Icons have rounded strokes. Time uses tabular numerals.

Derived details include a separate high-contrast focus ring, 44-pixel button targets where the layout allows, and container queries keyed to the player width. Playback progress is the only movement in the weave. Reduced motion disables decorative transitions. Keep the stock hotkeys, gestures, error dialog, and screen-reader announcements.

## Submission checks

Verify playback and essential controls on the public Vercel URL. Test narrow and wide player sizes, keyboard-only operation, Escape dismissal, visible focus, bright and dark footage, and reduced motion. Capture a submission visual that shows the illuminated timeline and an active seek preview. Claim the Mux bonus only for a rights-cleared video with our own public playback ID. Claim the live-site bonus only after verifying the skin on a genuine live site.

## Submission description

Afroclip Cinema brings the feeling of a small screening room to the web. An amber thread lights up across a geometric weave as the film plays, and scrubbing reveals chapter names when the film has them. The controls sit on their own charcoal rail so the picture keeps its full frame and the controls stay legible in any scene.
